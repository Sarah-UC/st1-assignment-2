# AI Usage Documentation

## AI Tool Used

I used Microsoft Copilot as the approved GenAI tool for Parts C and D of the SmartCare prototype activity.

## How AI Was Used

In Part C, I used Copilot as a Python tutor. I asked it to explain my appointment-booking code, identify three limitations, suggest improvements without rewriting the whole application, and ask questions to test my understanding.

In Part D, I asked Copilot to generate a simple alternative Python function for storing patient, practitioner, and appointment details using a list, dictionary, and function.

## How I Used the AI Output

I read and reviewed the explanations and generated code rather than accepting them without checking. I compared the AI-generated version with my own version to identify similarities and differences.

## Verification

I ran the AI-generated code and tested it using different cases, including a normal appointment, a blank patient name, duplicate appointments, and `None` values. The tests showed that the AI version accepted some invalid inputs and allowed duplicate appointments.

## My Contribution

I remained responsible for understanding the code, comparing the human and AI versions, testing their behaviour, identifying limitations, and deciding on the controlled improvement.
